how it works

Your ship fires on its own, always straight at the middle. Shots that miss ricochet off the wall and come back charged — red, worth double, and now lethal to you. Red rings kill on contact. The wall remembers every impact as a ripple.

128×128 pixels, drawn one at a time. No engine, no build step, no dependencies — open the file and it runs.
